Agriculture and food startup schemes

Agri and food startups have their own grant pipeline through agribusiness incubators at agricultural universities, plus dedicated funds and cheaper infrastructure loans.

11 schemes · Filter these in the full list

Central · ANRF / DST
Open

Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Fund

Up to 50% of project cost; loans at 3–4%

A ₹1 lakh crore, six-year fund for private R&D in strategic and sunrise sectors. Startups at technology readiness level (TRL) 4 and above get long-term, low-cost loans or equity through second-level fund managers — TDB for most sectors, BIRAC for biotech. The first cheques went out in May 2026.

Central · Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog
Periodic calls

Atal New India Challenge (ANIC 2.0)

Up to ₹1 crore

Grants for startups with a working prototype that solves a listed national challenge — in health, water, agriculture, food processing, housing, energy, mobility or space — to take it to market over 12–18 months.

All IndiaGrantChallenge / prize
Central · Ministry of Agriculture
Periodic calls

RKVY Agri-Startup Programme (formerly RKVY-RAFTAAR)

₹5 L (idea) + ₹25 L (seed)

The main central grant for agritech and allied startups — precision farming, farm machinery, food tech, supply chain, dairy, fisheries — delivered through agribusiness incubators at agricultural universities and institutes.

All IndiaGrantIncubationStudent-friendly
Central · Ministry of Food Processing Industries
Closes 30 Sep

PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises

35% subsidy, up to ₹10 lakh

A credit-linked capital subsidy for small food businesses — pickles, bakery, millets, spices, dairy products — to buy machinery and upgrade.

All IndiaSubsidyLoan
Central · NABARD
Open

AgriSURE Fund

Tickets up to ~₹25 cr

A blended fund for agri and rural startups: it invests in agri-focused AIFs and also directly in startups, from early stage to Series A.

All IndiaEquity
State · Dept of IT, Govt of Tripura
Open

Tripura Start-Up Policy 2024

₹2 L seed + ₹20k/month + ₹10 L prototype

Launched 24 January 2025 with a ₹50 crore VC fund (managed by SIDBI Venture Capital) and a ₹25 crore infrastructure fund.

TripuraGrantStipend
State · Dept of Industries, Govt of Himachal Pradesh
Open

Himachal CM Startup / Innovation Projects Scheme

₹25,000 a month for a year

Any individual or group with an innovative idea gets ₹25,000 a month for a year plus free incubation, mentoring and lab access at approved incubators. Himachal was a Top Performer in the 2026 rankings.

Periodic calls

PRIME Meghalaya Funding

Grants to ₹35 L · 0% loans to ₹75 L

Promotion and Incubation of Market-driven Enterprises: grants for early ideas and high-impact innovations, and interest-free loans for startups with traction.

Central · Ministry of Agriculture
Open

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund

Loans up to ₹2 cr with 3% interest subvention

Cheaper, guaranteed loans for post-harvest and farm infrastructure — warehouses, cold chains, sorting and grading units, primary processing — which agri startups can use too.

All IndiaLoanSubsidy
State · Dept of Commerce & Industry, Govt of Chhattisgarh
Open

Chhattisgarh Innovation & Startup Promotion Policy 2025-30

₹100 cr capital fund; seed, interest subsidy, risk-free loans

The 2025-30 policy offers seed funds, interest subsidies and risk-free loans across the startup lifecycle, with a ₹100 crore capital fund; idea-stage students are covered by a companion student startup policy.

The School Innovation Challenge (with UNICEF) for school students, Intinta Innovator for grassroots innovators, and TSIRI grants for rural-impact innovation.
