About

About this guide

An independent, searchable directory of government support for Indian startups.

What it covers

115 schemes: 66 run by central ministries, departments and government-backed bodies (SIDBI, NABARD, BIRAC, TDB and others), and 49 run by states and union territories. It covers grants, loans and credit guarantees, equity through government-backed funds, tax and compliance relief, IP support, incubation, and programmes that make government a customer. Student-focused schemes are included and tagged. Schemes reserved for specific founder groups are not covered in this edition.

How it's checked

Every entry was checked in September 2026 against the official portal, Press Information Bureau releases, Parliament replies or reputable news coverage. Each scheme page says when and how it was checked. Where only a secondary source was available, the page says so.

Amounts are maximums. What you actually receive depends on your project and the agency's appraisal. "Effort to apply" is the guide's own estimate, not an official rating.

Not an official website

This guide is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or run by any government body. Nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice. Always confirm current terms on the official portal linked from each scheme before applying, and speak to your chartered accountant for tax questions.

Privacy

There are no accounts, cookies or ads. Your shortlist, compare list, document ticks and finder answers are stored only in your own browser. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies or cross-site tracking.
