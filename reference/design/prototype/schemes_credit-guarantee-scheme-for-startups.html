Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups

The government guarantees most of a lender's risk, so banks, NBFCs and venture-debt funds can lend to startups without collateral. The cover limit was doubled to ₹20 crore per borrower in May 2025.

What you get

  • Guarantee up to ₹20 crore per borrower (was ₹10 crore)
  • 85% of default covered on loans up to ₹10 crore; 75% above that
  • Annual guarantee fee cut to 1% for startups in 27 Make in India champion sectors
  • Covers term loans, working capital and venture debt

Who can apply

  • DPIIT-recognised startup
  • Not in default to any lender
  • Loan from a scheduled bank, NBFC, all-India financial institution or SEBI-registered AIF

How to apply

  1. Approach your bank, NBFC or venture-debt fund and ask for a CGSS-covered loan
  2. The lender appraises you and applies to NCGTC for the guarantee
  3. Pay the annual guarantee fee (usually passed on by the lender)
