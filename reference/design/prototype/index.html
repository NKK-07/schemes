iDEX Defence Startup Challenges (DISC & Open Challenge)

Up to ₹1.5 crore

The Ministry of Defence's front door for startups. Solve a problem set by the armed forces, DRDO or defence PSUs and get a non-dilutive grant of up to ₹1.5 crore, trials with the users, and a path to procurement.

Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups

Collateral-free loans up to ₹20 cr

The government guarantees most of a lender's risk, so banks, NBFCs and venture-debt funds can lend to startups without collateral. The cover limit was doubled to ₹20 crore per borrower in May 2025.

All IndiaCredit guaranteeLoan
Central · Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises
Open

CGTMSE Collateral-Free MSME Loans

Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 cr

Banks lend to micro and small enterprises without collateral or third-party guarantees because CGTMSE covers most of the risk. The limit rose to ₹10 crore from April 2025.

All IndiaCredit guaranteeLoan
Central · Ministry of MSME
Open

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme

15–35% subsidy on projects up to ₹50 L

A capital subsidy on bank loans for new micro enterprises: up to ₹50 lakh project cost for manufacturing and ₹20 lakh for services, with 15–35% of the cost given as margin money subsidy.

All IndiaSubsidyLoanStudent-friendly
Central · Dept of Financial Services
Open

PM Mudra Yojana (incl. Tarun Plus)

Collateral-free loans ₹50,000–₹20 lakh

Small collateral-free business loans in four tiers: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (up to ₹5 lakh), Tarun (up to ₹10 lakh) and Tarun Plus (₹10–20 lakh) for those who have repaid a Tarun loan.

All IndiaLoanStudent-friendly
State · Kerala Startup Mission
Open

KSUM Innovation Grants (Idea, Productisation, Scale-up)

₹3 L idea · ₹7 L product · ₹15 L scale-up

Kerala's staged grants follow a startup from idea to revenue: an Idea Grant for prototypes, a Productisation Grant to finish the product, and a Scale-up Grant once you have revenue or investment.

KeralaGrantStudent-friendly
Central · DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry
Open

DPIIT Startup Recognition

Free · gateway to most benefits

The official 'startup' tag. Most central benefits — tax holiday, CGSS, fund-of-funds money, IPR rebates and procurement relaxations — only unlock once you hold this certificate. The February 2026 framework doubled the turnover limit and created a new Deep Tech Startup category.

All IndiaRegistration & complianceStudent-friendly

Open

IndiaAI Compute (subsidised GPUs)

38,000+ GPUs at subsidised hourly rates

Under the ₹10,372 crore IndiaAI Mission, Indian startups, researchers and students can rent high-end GPUs through the AI compute portal at heavily subsidised rates, instead of paying global cloud prices.

All IndiaComputeSubsidyStudent-friendly
Central · ANRF / DST
Open

Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Fund

Up to 50% of project cost; loans at 3–4%

A ₹1 lakh crore, six-year fund for private R&D in strategic and sunrise sectors. Startups at technology readiness level (TRL) 4 and above get long-term, low-cost loans or equity through second-level fund managers — TDB for most sectors, BIRAC for biotech. The first cheques went out in May 2026.

All IndiaLoanEquity
Central · DPIIT
Open

Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0

₹10,000 cr corpus via VC funds

The government doesn't invest in startups directly; it commits capital to SEBI-registered venture funds (AIFs), which then invest in DPIIT-recognised startups. Version 2.0 (approved 14 Feb 2026; guidelines 25 Apr 2026) earmarks money for deep tech, micro-VC funds for early-growth startups, and tech-led manufacturing.

All IndiaEquity
Central · DPIIT & Office of CGPDTM
Open

Startup IP Protection (SIPP): patent & trademark fee rebates

80% off patent fees, 50% off trademark fees

Recognised startups file patents at a fraction of the normal cost, get free help from empanelled facilitators, and can ask for expedited examination — often cutting grant time from years to months.

All IndiaIP supportStudent-friendly
Central · CBDT & DPIIT Inter-Ministerial Board
Open

Startup Tax Holiday (Section 140, formerly 80-IAC)

100% profit deduction for 3 years

Eligible startups pay no income tax on profits for any 3 consecutive years out of their first 10. Since 1 April 2026 the benefit sits in Section 140 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (it was Section 80-IAC under the 1961 Act).

All IndiaTax relief

Up to ₹1 crore

Grants for startups with a working prototype that solves a listed national challenge — in health, water, agriculture, food processing, housing, energy, mobility or space — to take it to market over 12–18 months.
