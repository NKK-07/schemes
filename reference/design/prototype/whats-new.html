What changed

The last twelve months, in order

New schemes, closures and changed limits that affect startups.

  1. Sep 2026

    Semicon 2.0 revamps chip-design support

    MeitY notified new DLI terms: VC co-investment, royalty financing and a 9% deployment incentive (up to ₹30 crore).

    Open DLI →
  2. Jul 2026

    Delhi approves ₹400 crore Start-up Policy 2026

    Runs through universities, colleges, polytechnics and ITIs; targets 10,000 startups.

    Open Delhi Policy →
  3. Jun 2026

    Punjab raises seed grant to ₹5 lakh

    Up from ₹3 lakh under the Punjab Startup & Industrial Policy 2026.

    Open Punjab Policy →
  4. May 2026

    Startup India Seed Fund closes

    SISFS stopped taking startup applications after two extensions; no successor announced yet.

    Open SISFS →
  5. May 2026

    First RDI Fund cheques released

    Five deep-tech startups funded through TDB and BIRAC, including ₹105 crore to a space startup.

    Open RDI Fund →
  6. Apr 2026

    Fund of Funds 2.0 guidelines issued

    ₹10,000 crore for deep tech, micro-VC, manufacturing and sector-agnostic funds; SIDBI implements.

    Open FoF 2.0 →
  7. Apr 2026

    80-IAC becomes Section 140

    The Income-tax Act, 2025 took effect; the startup tax holiday continues under a new section number.

    Open Sec 140 →
  8. Mar 2026

    Mutual credit guarantee eased for manufacturers

    MCGS-MSME now covers 75% for exporters and needs equipment to be only 60% of project cost.

    Open MCGS-MSME →
  9. Feb 2026

    Railways launches Rail Tech Policy

    iDEX-style portal; up to 50% funding and bigger prototype and scale-up grants.

    Open Rail Tech →
  10. Feb 2026

    New DPIIT startup definition

    Turnover limit doubled to ₹200 crore; new Deep Tech Startup category (20 years, ₹300 crore); co-ops eligible.

    Open DPIIT →
  11. Feb 2026

    Budget 2026-27 for small firms

    ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund, ₹2,000 crore SRI Fund top-up, Biopharma SHAKTI, TReDS push and Corporate Mitras.

    Open SME Growth Fund →
  12. Jan 2026

    Karnataka Startup Policy 2025-30 launched

    ₹675 crore outlay; ELEVATE NxT deep-tech grants up to ₹1 crore.

    Open KA Policy →
  13. Jan 2026

    5th States' Startup Ranking released

    Gujarat best among large states; Arunachal Pradesh and Goa best among small states and UTs.

    See state schemes →
  14. Oct 2025

    PRIP opens for pharma and medtech

    Startups can get up to ₹5 crore for early-stage projects.

    Open PRIP →
  15. Sep 2025

    Goa rolls out Startup Policy 2025

    Adds a Campus Innovation Scheme for students.

    Open Goa Policy →
  16. Aug 2025

    Maharashtra approves 2025 startup policy

    ₹500 crore Maha-Fund and ₹25 lakh pilot orders via Startup Week.

    Open MH Policy 2025 →
  17. May 2025

    CGSS cover doubled to ₹20 crore

    Lower 1% fee for startups in 27 champion sectors.

    Open CGSS →
