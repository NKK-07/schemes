Glossary

The acronyms, decoded

Dotted-underlined terms anywhere in the guide link here.

AIF
Alternative Investment Fund — a SEBI-registered pooled fund, such as a venture capital fund.
AIFs
Alternative Investment Funds — SEBI-registered pooled funds, such as venture capital funds.
AIM
Atal Innovation Mission, run by NITI Aayog.
angel tax
The old tax on share premium above fair value, abolished from AY 2025-26.
ANRF
Anusandhan National Research Foundation — manages the RDI Fund.
BIRAC
Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council — DBT's funding arm for biotech startups.
CGTMSE
Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises — guarantees collateral-free loans to small firms.
convertible debentures
Debt that can later convert into equity shares.
CPSE
Central public sector enterprise — a company owned by the central government.
DBT
Department of Biotechnology.
DIC
District Industries Centre — the district office for industry schemes.
DPIIT
Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade — the central department that recognises startups and runs Startup India.
DPR
Detailed project report — a costed plan banks and agencies use to appraise a project.
DST
Department of Science and Technology.
EDA
Electronic design automation — software used to design chips.
EIR
Entrepreneur in Residence — a stipend to work full time on a startup idea.
EMD
Earnest money deposit — a security deposit normally required to bid in tenders.
GeM
Government e-Marketplace — the online portal through which government departments buy.
IIC
Institution's Innovation Council — the innovation cell in a college.
IMB
Inter-Ministerial Board — the panel that certifies startups for the income-tax holiday.
IPR
Intellectual property rights — patents, trademarks, designs, copyright.
KSUM
Kerala Startup Mission — Kerala's startup agency.
KVIC
Khadi and Village Industries Commission — implements PMEGP.
MeitY
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
MSInS
Maharashtra State Innovation Society — Maharashtra's startup agency.
MSME
Micro, small and medium enterprise.
MVP
Minimum viable product — the simplest version customers can use.
NABARD
National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development.
NBFC
Non-banking financial company — an RBI-regulated lender that isn't a bank.
NCGTC
National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company — runs government credit guarantee schemes such as CGSS.
NIDHI
National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations — DST's umbrella programme for startup support.
PoC
Proof of concept — evidence that your core idea works.
QRate
iStart Rajasthan's startup rating, needed for larger state incentives.
R-ABI
RKVY Agribusiness Incubator — agri incubators that run the RKVY startup grants.
SEBI
Securities and Exchange Board of India — the markets regulator that registers VC funds.
SGST
State Goods and Services Tax — the state's share of GST, which some states refund to startups.
SIDBI
Small Industries Development Bank of India — the lead development bank for MSMEs and the manager of several startup funds.
SLFM
Second Level Fund Manager — an agency (like TDB or BIRAC) that deploys RDI Fund money to companies.
SPOC
Single point of contact — the coordinator at your institution.
sustenance allowance
A monthly stipend some states pay founders so they can work on their startup full time.
TBI
Technology Business Incubator — an incubator, usually at an academic institution, supported by DST.
TDB
Technology Development Board — a DST body that lends to and invests in technology companies.
TReDS
Trade Receivables Discounting System — platforms where small firms sell invoices for early payment.
TRL
Technology Readiness Level — a 1–9 scale; TRL 4 means a technology validated in the lab.
Udyam
The official MSME registration, done online with Aadhaar and PAN.
venture debt
Loans to startups, usually alongside equity rounds, from specialised lenders.
