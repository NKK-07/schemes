Beginner's roadmap

New to government schemes? Go in this order.

Each step unlocks the next — recognition opens tax and credit benefits, incubation opens grants, and a working product opens seed money and debt.

For foundersFor students

For founders

  1. Incorporate the company

    Register a private limited company or LLP through the MCA portal. Most schemes need an incorporated entity.

  2. Get DPIIT recognition and Udyam registration

    Both are free and online. DPIIT unlocks central startup benefits; Udyam unlocks MSME loans, guarantees and procurement preferences.

    DPIITFree · gateway to most benefitsUdyamFree · unlocks MSME benefits

  3. Register with your state's startup portal

    State allowances, seed grants and reimbursements need state registration. Open the map to find yours.

  4. Join an incubator

    Incubators are the doorway to PRAYAS, EIR, NIDHI-SSP, TIDE and many state grants.

    IncubatorsSubsidised space, labs and…

  5. Protect your IP cheaply

    File patents and trademarks as a recognised startup to get the fee rebates.

    SIPP80% off patent fees, 50% off…

  6. Fund the prototype with grants

    Idea-stage grants don't dilute you. Pick those that match your sector.

    PRAYASUp to ₹10 lakhTIDE 2.0EIR up to ₹4 L; grant up to ₹7 LBIGUp to ₹50 lakhRKVY₹5 L (idea) + ₹25 L (seed)

  7. Take the product to market

    Seed grants, accelerators and challenges for startups with a working product.

    NIDHI-SSPUp to ₹1 croreSAMRIDHUp to ₹40 lakhANICUp to ₹1 croreiDEXUp to ₹1.5 crore

  8. Add debt without collateral

    Guaranteed loans let you borrow for working capital and equipment without pledging assets.

    CGSSCollateral-free loans up to ₹20 crCGTMSECollateral-free loans up to ₹10 crMudraCollateral-free loans…

  9. Raise equity

    Pitch VC funds backed by the government's Fund of Funds, or sector funds for space and agri.

    FoF 2.0₹10,000 cr corpus via VC fundsAgriSURETickets up to ~₹25 crSpace VC Fund₹1,000 cr fund

  10. Sell to government and claim the tax holiday

    Use procurement relaxations on GeM, and apply for the IMB certificate once you're profitable.

    Join your college's innovation cell and ask about startup credits, leave and IP policy.

    Hackathons and challenges build a team, a prototype and credibility.
